How can we help you today?

Account & Security

Is my financial data secure?

Absolutely. We use bank-level 256-bit TLS encryption for all data transmission and partner with industry-leading aggregators to handle bank connections securely. We also support multi-factor authentication to protect your account. For more details, please visit our Security page.

How do I reset my password?

If you've forgotten your password, you can click the "Forgot password?" link on the login page. You will receive an email with instructions on how to reset it.

How do I change my email address?

You can change your email address from the 'Profile' section in your account settings. For security reasons, you will be asked to confirm this change via an email sent to your new address.

Connecting Accounts

How do I link my bank account?

To link your bank account, navigate to the 'Accounts' section in your dashboard and click 'Add Account'. You will be prompted to select your bank and securely log in using your bank credentials through our trusted third-party partner. Your credentials are never stored on our servers.

Can I track my investments?

Yes! You can connect your brokerage and crypto accounts in the 'Investments' section. This will give you a complete overview of your portfolio, performance, and asset allocation, all in one place.

What if my bank is not listed?

We are constantly working to add support for more financial institutions. If your bank is not listed, please contact our support team with the name of your bank, and we will do our best to prioritize its addition.

Using Financify

How do I create a budget?

You can create a budget from the 'Budgeting' tab. Select 'Create New Budget', choose a category (e.g., Groceries, Transport), and set a monthly limit. Financify will automatically track your spending in that category against your budget.

How are my transactions categorized?

Financify uses a smart categorization engine that automatically assigns a category to your transactions based on the merchant name. You can always manually change the category for any transaction to better suit your needs.

Does Financify have a mobile app?

Currently, Financify is a web-based platform fully optimized for mobile browsers. A dedicated mobile app for iOS and Android is in development and will be released soon!
