Powerful Tools for Your Financial Life

Dive deep into the features that make Financify the most comprehensive financial companion you'll ever need.

Smart Budgeting & Insights

Take the guesswork out of budgeting. Create personalized budgets for categories like groceries, dining out, and shopping in just a few clicks. Financify automatically categorizes your spending as it happens, giving you a real-time view of where your money is going.

Our smart insights engine analyzes your habits and provides helpful tips. We'll send you alerts when you're approaching a budget limit, so you can adjust your spending and stay on track. Visualize your progress with beautiful, interactive charts that make understanding your finances simple and even enjoyable.

Bank-Level Security

Your trust and security are paramount. We've engineered Financify from the ground up with multiple layers of protection for your sensitive data. All communication is secured with 256-bit TLS encryption, the same standard used by major banks and financial institutions.

We never store your bank login credentials on our servers. Instead, we partner with industry-leading data aggregators that use secure token-based authentication. Plus, with support for multi-factor authentication (MFA) and biometric login (Face ID, Touch ID), you can be confident that your account is protected against unauthorized access.

Investment Portfolio Tracking

See your entire investment portfolio in one place. Securely connect your brokerage accounts, retirement funds, and even cryptocurrency wallets to get a holistic view of your net worth. Track your holdings, see daily gains and losses, and monitor your asset allocation over time.

Financify helps you stay informed with real-time market data and performance charts. Compare your portfolio's growth against major market indices and understand how all your investments are working together to build your wealth. It's the big-picture view you need to make smarter investment decisions.
